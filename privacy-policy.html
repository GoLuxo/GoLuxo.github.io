Privacy policy

FlashDrive Boot Maker · Last updated: November 9, 2026

Summary

FlashDrive Boot Maker writes disk images to USB drives entirely on your device. We don't have accounts, we don't run servers that receive your files, and we never upload your images or drive contents anywhere.

Files and USB drives

The image you pick is read only on your phone, through Android's file picker, and only to write it to the USB drive you choose. USB access is granted per drive by you through Android's permission dialog.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) we ask for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google/Privacy → Ads. Learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows flashing progress and the result. Nothing is sent off the device.

Data we store

Your settings, the names of recently used image files and a history of drives you have flashed are stored only on your device, so you can re-flash quickly. Uninstalling the app deletes them, and you can clear the history at any time in the History tab. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

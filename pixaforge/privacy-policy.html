Privacy policy

PixaForge: Pixel Art Maker · Afton Studio · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Summary

PixaForge is a pixel art and animation editor. Your artworks stay on your device. We don't have accounts, we don't run servers that receive your drawings, and we never upload your art anywhere.

Your artwork

Projects, thumbnails and optional reference photos are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Files you export or share are sent only to the place you choose (your gallery, Downloads, or an app you pick in the share sheet).

Camera and photos

When you choose "Camera" or "Photo", the photo is opened only on your device to convert it to pixel art or to show it as a drawing reference. The app uses Android's photo picker and your camera app; it does not request access to your whole photo library and does not upload images.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Some exports and premium palettes are unlocked by watching a rewarded video ad. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) we ask for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time from the app menu → Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google/Privacy → Ads. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Data we store

Your settings (such as grid preferences, recent colors and unlocked palettes) are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Android may include your projects in your personal device backup if you have backup turned on. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

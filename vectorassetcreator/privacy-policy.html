Privacy policy

Vector Asset Creator · Afton Studio · Last updated: November 23, 2026

Summary

Vector Asset Creator is an editor for vector icons and graphics (Android VectorDrawable, SVG, Jetpack Compose and PNG). Your designs stay on your device. There are no accounts, we don't run servers that receive your files, and we never upload your artwork.

Your designs and files

Projects are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. When you import an SVG or XML file, the app reads only the file you pick in Android's file picker. Files you export or share are sent only to the place you choose (a folder you pick, or an app you select in the share sheet). Text you copy goes to your device clipboard.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your photos, camera, contacts or location. It uses the Internet permission only so Google AdMob can load ads.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, including a short rewarded video before exports. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction and diagnostics data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) we ask for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time from the app menu → Privacy & ad choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google/Privacy → Ads. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Data we store

Settings such as grid preferences and recent colors are stored only on your device; uninstalling the app deletes them. Android may include your projects in your personal device backup if you have backup turned on. We do not collect, sell or share personal data ourselves.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

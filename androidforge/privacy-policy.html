Android Forge Studio – Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026 · Afton Studio

Android Forge Studio ("the app") lets you design, program and build Android apps on your phone. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: your projects and signing keys stay on your device, we do not run servers that collect your data, and we have no user accounts.

Information stored on your device

  • Projects – screens, blocks, settings and the images, sounds, fonts and files you add are saved in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Signing keys – keys you create or import (and their passwords) are stored in the app's private storage so the app can sign your builds. They are excluded from cloud backups. They never leave your device unless you choose to back them up or share them.
  • Builds – APK, AAB and exported project files are created on your device. They are only shared when you use Share or Save.

We (Afton Studio) cannot see any of this content. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interaction data. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time from the app menu ("Ad privacy choices"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Subscribers to Forge Studio Pro see no ads.

Purchases

Forge Studio Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never receive your card details. The app only checks with Google Play whether your subscription is active. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state – loading ads, checking your subscription, and letting the apps you preview use web features you add.
  • Downloads – when you tap “Save & install”, the app you built is saved to your Downloads folder so you can install it with Android’s own installer.
  • Vibrate and notifications – used only when an app you are previewing uses vibration or notification blocks.

Apps you build

Apps you create with Android Forge Studio are your own. If you publish them, you are responsible for their content, their permissions and their own privacy policies. Apps built on the free plan show a short "Made with Android Forge Studio" badge at launch; they contain no ads or tracking from us.

Children

Android Forge Studio is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and do not share it with third parties other than the Google services described above.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its effective date.
